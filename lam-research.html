Lam Research LRCX

Lam Research sells the etch and deposition tools behind the AI build-out. Memory share gains and a larger, higher-margin installed base justify a premium. After a c.20% fall since late September, the shares trade below our base-case value ($349), so the upside no longer depends on the bull case.

Investment Thesis

  • The build-out precedes the proof: AI capacity must be built in silicon first, and that capacity is built with Lam's etch and deposition tools.
  • Share gains in memory: within the Lam/AMAT/TEL peer set, Lam gained DRAM share in 1H26 and took most of the NAND increment in the June-2026 quarter.
  • A larger, higher-margin installed base: customer support (CSBG) growth and operating leverage support margin expansion as the systems cycle normalises.

Potential Catalysts

  • 1Q FY27 results (21 October 2026) versus the $8.1 bn guidance midpoint.
  • Continued memory share gains (NAND conversions, DRAM/HBM).
  • Recovery of foundry/logic share versus Applied Materials in GAA.
  • Progress towards management's long-term margin targets.
  • CY2027 WFE outlook and TSMC capex guidance.

Key Risks

  • A capex pause by foundries and memory makers after the first AI capacity wave.
  • Multiple de-rating towards historical cycle averages.
  • Applied Materials competition in gate-all-around steps.
  • China export controls (c.34% of FY26 revenue).
  • Memory cycle: a return to commodity capacity additions.
