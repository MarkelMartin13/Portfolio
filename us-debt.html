U.S. Debt Situation MACRO

The analysis examines the U.S. refinancing challenge, rising net interest costs, foreign demand for Treasuries and the narrowing margin between the effective cost of debt and nominal economic growth.

Download Full Research ↓

Investment Thesis

  • Large refinancing needs increase exposure to prevailing market rates.
  • Debt sustainability depends on the relationship between average interest cost, nominal growth and the primary deficit.
  • Foreign demand, especially Japanese investor behaviour, can affect Treasury absorption and term premia.

Potential Catalysts

  • Lower inflation allowing lower policy and market rates.
  • Improved Treasury market absorption and lower term premia.
  • Stronger nominal growth widening the growth-versus-interest-cost margin.
  • A reduction in the primary deficit.

Key Risks

  • Persistently high yields raising refinancing costs.
  • Weaker nominal growth.
  • Reduced foreign demand for Treasuries.
  • Large fiscal deficits and rising net interest expense.
  • A feedback loop from greater issuance to higher risk premia.
