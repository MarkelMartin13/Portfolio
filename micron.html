Micron MU

Micron offers cyclical exposure to a recovery in memory markets together with a structural AI demand catalyst. The thesis depends on improving supply-demand conditions, pricing discipline and higher-value memory products.

Download Research Summary ↓

Investment Thesis

  • Memory pricing can recover as supply remains disciplined.
  • AI servers require significantly more high-performance memory.
  • Operating leverage can materially improve earnings during the upcycle.

Potential Catalysts

  • DRAM pricing recovery
  • HBM demand
  • AI data-centre investment

Key Risks

  • Semiconductor cycle reversal
  • New supply additions
  • Customer concentration
