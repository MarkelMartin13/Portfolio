COMPANY ANALYSIS

Microsoft MSFT

Microsoft combines a resilient enterprise software franchise with a rapidly expanding cloud platform. The investment thesis is centred on Azure growth, monetisation of AI services and the durability of recurring enterprise revenue.

Download Research Summary ↓

Investment Thesis

  • Azure remains the principal structural growth engine.
  • AI products can increase revenue per customer across the Microsoft ecosystem.
  • Recurring software and cloud revenue support strong cash generation.

Potential Catalysts

  • Accelerating Azure growth
  • Copilot monetisation
  • Operating leverage from scale

Key Risks

  • Higher AI infrastructure spending
  • Cloud competition
  • Slower enterprise IT budgets
