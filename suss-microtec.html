SUSS MicroTec SMHN

SUSS MicroTec makes the equipment that prepares wafers for the advanced packaging of AI chips: temporary bonders for HBM memory and coaters for packaging. 2026 is the sales trough, but orders have already turned: the backlog reached a record €473.7m in June 2026, with about €220m already scheduled for 2027.

Updated 18 October 2026 · Price €56.35 (Xetra close, 16-Oct-2026) · Target price €84.9 (probability-weighted, 6–18 months) · Upside +50.6% · Report in Spanish

Download Full Research ↓

Investment Thesis

  • Orders have already turned: book-to-bill rose from 0.70x in 2025 to 1.73x in Q1 2026 and 2.24x in Q2 2026; orders convert into sales with a 2–4 quarter lag.
  • 2027–2028 are the sales years: we model €644m in 2027 and €864m in 2028 (from €457m in 2026), with EBIT margin rising from 8.7% to 20.2%.
  • The market does not believe it: SUSS trades at 14x 2027e EPS and 8x 2027e EBITDA, less than half its advanced-packaging peers (c.30x P/E).

Potential Catalysts

  • Q3 2026 statement (5 November 2026): order intake, backlog and gross margin.
  • FY2026 results and 2027 guidance (March 2027).
  • A first production order in hybrid bonding with an HBM maker.
  • Advanced-packaging capex at TSMC and OSATs (CoWoS capacity).

Key Risks

  • A pause in memory (HBM) capex: Advanced Backend orders back to 2025 levels.
  • Unfavourable product mix: more coaters and fewer temporary bonders.
  • Delivery and acceptance delays shifting sales from 2027 to 2028.
  • HBM moving to wafer-to-wafer hybrid bonding in the long term.
  • China and geopolitics; dilution from the approved convertible programme.
