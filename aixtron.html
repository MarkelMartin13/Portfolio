Aixtron AIXA

Aixtron makes the MOCVD reactors that grow the crystal layers of compound semiconductors: the lasers that connect AI data centres and the GaN and SiC power chips that feed them. The thesis depends on the build-out continuing, not on AI proving profitable.

Updated 18 October 2026 · Price €32.89 (Xetra close, 16-Oct-2026) · Target price €46.0 (probability-weighted, 6–18 months) · Upside +39.9% · Report in Spanish

Download Full Research ↓

Investment Thesis

  • Short term: optoelectronics is already accelerating — laser makers have multiplied capex and Aixtron is capturing most of the epitaxy growth in its peer group.
  • Medium term: power takes over — 800V DC data centres need more efficient conversion, and GaN fab utilisation points to new tool orders in 2027.
  • The market prices only the optical cycle: our 2028 revenue estimates are 8–12% above consensus, mainly on a faster GaN recovery.

Potential Catalysts

  • Q3 2026 statement (29 October 2026): order intake in optoelectronics and comments on GaN and SiC.
  • Coherent and Lumentum capex updates.
  • FY2026 results and 2027 guidance (March 2027).
  • Start-up of the Penang (Malaysia) plant.
  • First 800V DC deployments and GaN multi-tool orders (H1 2027).

Key Risks

  • GaN and SiC recovery is delayed while optoelectronics enters digestion.
  • End of the laser shortage and double ordering.
  • Lower AI data-centre investment.
  • Gross margin below plan as the mix shifts to power.
  • Competition (Veeco in 300 mm GaN, AMEC in China), FX and convertible dilution.
